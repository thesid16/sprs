% f11_export_cost variant c -- absolute time, not a ratio.
% Data: experiments/w_final/W2/v004/COST.json, cost_table fields stock_us and
%   fbt_us, rows backend=nccl, G=2, dtype=fp64, N=8, both arms; scalars from
%   data/wround_macros.tex.  The excesses quoted in the annotations are
%   fbt_us - stock_us at each size: 131, 67, 92, 63 us at 8 KiB to 512 KiB,
%   then 274, 373, 1761, 13026 us at 2 MiB to 128 MiB.
% Strategy: drop the ratio entirely. A ratio hides that the worst overheads
%   are attached to the cheapest calls -- at \PinSmallSize{} the whole stock
%   collective is \PinSmallStock~us, so a \PinSmallCost{}x overhead is about
%   130 us of fixed launch cost, not 130 us of lost bandwidth. Plotting
%   microseconds shows stock flat while pinned pays a size-independent
%   surcharge, and lets a reader price the export in the units they budget in.
% Scope: NCCL only; the gloo rows in the same file are excluded as a broken
%   baseline (stock gloo at G=24 is \PinGlooBadRatio{}x SLOWER on
%   \PinGlooBadLo{} than on \PinGlooBadHi{}).
