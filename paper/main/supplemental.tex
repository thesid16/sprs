\section{The Fifteen Assumptions}\label{app:assumptions}
% =====================================================================

Five compiler obligations, discharged by the compiler or the user; ten fabric
invariants, established by RTL inspection. The four marked~$\dagger$ are
hypotheses of Property~\ref{main-prop:2} only: violating one can stop a run but
cannot alter a root the fabric does produce.

\emph{Compiler obligations.}
\textsf{C-sw} (single-writer allocation for remote destinations): partitioning
each PE's DMEM into \emph{receive buffers} --- addresses named as
\texttt{target\_addr} by a remote \texttt{SEND} --- and \emph{local
temporaries}, the image contains for every PE $p$ and receive buffer
$a \neq 0$ exactly one write to $(p,a)$, and it is remote. Local temporaries
are exempt: the allocator reuses them and their safety comes from in-order
issue rather than single assignment. The scoping is load-bearing and the two
classes are disjoint by construction; on the emitted images of
\RecycleConfigs{} configurations the recycled and remotely-written address
sets never intersect, and no operand is read after the write that recycles its
slot (\RecycleUnsafe{} such events in \RecycleEvents{}).
\textsf{C-bind} and \textsf{C-leaf} are Defs.~\ref{main-def:opbind}
and~\ref{main-def:leafbind}, checked as post-conditions on the emitted image.
\textsf{C-mem} (memory feasibility): every PE names at most
$\sv{DMEM\_DEPTH} = \DmemDepth$ distinct DMEM addresses and every emitted
router id is below the adjacency sentinel (\RouterIdBits{} bits, at most
\RouterIdMax). Neither is enforced at run time --- the fabric truncates DMEM
and \texttt{SEND} target addresses to \SendAddrBits{} bits, so an over-budget
image aliases two addresses onto one slot silently, and out-of-range router
ids alias onto low ids, which is what stopped one instance from ever settling
(\S\ref{main-sec:limitations}, L0) --- but both are cheap to discharge, peak
occupancy being an $O(N)$ read-off the allocator already maintains.
$\dagger$\,\textsf{C-route}: the emitted tables encode destination-correct,
loop-free paths. We claim those two properties only; the tables are
minimal-distance rather than dimension-ordered, so we do not claim an acyclic
channel-dependency graph.

\emph{Fabric invariants.}
\textsf{F-sb-mono}: for every receive buffer $a$, \texttt{scoreboard[a]}
transitions $0\to1$ at most once per program, at the clock edge of the unique
remote write, and stays set until reset.
\textsf{F-sb-gate}: \texttt{COMPUTE} stalls \textsf{DE}$\to$\textsf{EX} until
both operand bits are $1$, \texttt{SEND} until its source bit is.
\textsf{F-fw}: a DMEM read at cycle $t$ returns the value committed by the
unique write at cycle $\leq t$, even across the adjacent-cycle race.
\textsf{F-inorder}: each PE issues in order through
\textsf{IF}$\to$\textsf{DE}$\to$\textsf{EX}, one instruction at a time,
sampling both operands at the \textsf{DE} read port strictly before writing
back in \textsf{EX} --- which is what makes local-temporary reuse safe, since
the scoreboard cannot, a recycled slot's bit being already set.
\textsf{F-gci-haz}: from \sv{gci\_start} until the GCI agent commits,
\textsf{DE} stalls any \texttt{COMPUTE} or \texttt{SEND} naming the pending
destination.
\textsf{F-arb}: strict priority $\mathrm{PIU} > \mathrm{GCI} > \mathrm{FSM}$
with a GCI retry latch; no write is silently dropped.
\textsf{F-fadd}: \sv{fp64\_add.sv} is combinational and stateless, its output
a pure function of the two 64-bit inputs sampled at the EX clock edge.
$\dagger$\,\textsf{F-credit}: per link and interval,
$\#\mathrm{credit\_returns} \leq \#\mathrm{buffer\_reads}$ with bounded wait.
$\dagger$\,\textsf{F-misroute}: packets whose \sv{dest\_gpu\_id} does not
match the local PE are dropped, not silently consumed.
$\dagger$\,\textsf{F-watchdog}: a stall exceeding
$\sv{WATCHDOG\_MAX} = \WatchdogMax$ cycles raises \texttt{P\_ERROR}, as does
an invalid opcode immediately.

Table~\ref{tab:catalogue} lists the eighteen assignment algorithms, ordered
by family and deliberately not by membership: below the top four they are not
distinguishable from one another on a suite of this size and should be read as
a block. A zero share does not mean an algorithm is useless --- it means the
algorithm never matched the instance minimum here, and several zero-share
algorithms are close seconds. Where an algorithm is not evaluable on every
instance the denominator is the eligible subset; \sv{ExactBB} in particular is
size-gated beyond $N \approx 64$, so its figure reflects unavailability rather
than defeat. The mapping stage is absent by design: all ten mapping algorithms
attain the champion cycle count on \ClsAllMappings{} of \TournNInst{}
instances, so no per-mapping share is reportable. Provenance for the
implementations from the literature: list scheduling follows HEFT and
CPOP~\cite{topcuoglu2002heft} and Hu's level-based bound~\cite{hu1961parallel};
tree dynamic programming follows Lukes~\cite{lukes1974efficient}; clustering
follows DSC~\cite{yang1994dsc}; multilevel and graph partitioning follow
Karypis and Kumar~\cite{karypis1998multilevel} with Fiduccia--Mattheyses
refinement~\cite{fiduccia1982linear}. The remainder are our own constructions
and are marked as such in the released source. Table~\ref{tab:coverage} gives
the coverage the suite achieves; its unevenness is what bounds every
scale-dependent statement in \S\ref{main-sec:selrule}.

\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}
% =====================================================================

\emph{Artefacts.} The ten fabric sources of \S\ref{main-sec:results}, the
compiler and its testbench generator, the mutation harness for both injected
classes, the pinned-FBT implementation of \S\ref{main-sec:pinfbt} and the result
files behind every table and figure are released with the paper and will be
deposited under a persistent identifier; the three FPGA bring-up wrappers need
IP and board constraints we do not redistribute. Everything reported here was
tested under Vivado xsim 2025.2 on Linux. The compiler is Python~3.9+,
deterministic under insertion-ordered dict semantics, with RNG seeds pinned at
$42$.

\emph{What each verdict is.} The per-run verdict is the emitted testbench's
comparison of the fabric's result against the canonical root carried as a
compile-time \sv{EXPECTED} constant. The \textsf{C-*} obligations are a
static audit over the released images (\S\ref{main-sec:compiler}), not checks that
ran inside the compile loop. We ship no SystemVerilog assertion suite and no
claim here rests on one.

\emph{The oracle is not independent, and this is where that is recorded.}
The Python $\fadd$ mirror is a transcription of \sv{fp64\_add.sv}, not an
independent model: it is bit-identical to the repaired RTL on
\FpAcceptVectors{} vectors (\FpAcceptMismatch{} mismatches), and on the
as-submitted pair --- the negative control --- the two agree with each other
while both disagree with correctly-rounded addition on \FpCtrlErrors{} of
\FpCtrlVectors{} vectors (\S\ref{main-sec:fabric-fadd}, L13).

\emph{Reproducing a root.} Leaf $i$ carries $L_i$ from~\eqref{main-eq:leaves}, and
every HW-validated configuration at the same $N$ produces a $64$-bit root
whose hash matches the corresponding column of Table~\ref{main-tab:invariance}.
