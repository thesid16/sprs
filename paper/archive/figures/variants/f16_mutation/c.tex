% Strategy: coverage and its shape. Variants a and b give the aggregate route

%   relative to where the evidence is worth most, three seeds at the four small

%   The stack is deliberately by CLASS, not by detection route: the route is a
%   static reconstruction over the whole campaign (\MutAddrStall{} fail-stops,
%   at most \MutLiveSlotMax{} live-slot hits) and is not recorded per N, so a
%   per-N route stack would be invented. The caption says so.
